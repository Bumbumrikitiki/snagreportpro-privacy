Privacy Policy – SnagReport Pro

This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected and processed by SnagReport Pro and for what purposes.

1. General information

SnagReport Pro is a mobile application used to create inspection reports, store room data, photos, comments, and defect notes. The app primarily works locally on the user's device and does not require an account.

2. Data collected and processed

a) Data stored locally on the device

The app may store the following information on the user's device: report names, building and floor information, client, contractor, and inspector details, dates, deadlines, and inspection status, general notes, room names, photo descriptions and notes, uploaded photos, draft and final report versions.

These data are stored locally on the device and are used to create, edit, and save inspection reports offline.

b) Advertising-related data

If the app displays ads through Google AdMob, the advertising network may process the following information: advertising identifier, device type and model, operating system, device language, country or region, ad impressions, clicks, and interactions, technical information necessary to serve ads.

Such data are processed by Google AdMob according to Google's privacy policies and advertising requirements.

c) No user account required

The app does not require user registration, login, email, phone number, or account creation.

3. Purpose of data processing

Data are processed to: create and save inspection reports, store photos and room information, enable offline work, display advertisements in accordance with advertising network requirements, support the correct functioning of the app and error handling.

4. Storage of data

Most user data are stored locally on the device in the app's local storage. The app does not send these data to an external server operated by the developer unless the user chooses to export a report file or back it up outside the app.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell user data. We do not share user data with third parties, except where required by law or necessary for advertising services such as Google AdMob.

6. Advertising and advertising tools

The app may use Google AdMob advertising services. Ads may be shown in personalized or non-personalized form depending on applicable regulations and user privacy settings.

7. User rights

Users may request: access to data stored within the app, deletion of locally stored data, withdrawal of consent to personalized advertising where applicable, deletion of data where such functionality is available in the app.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect user data stored on the device. However, the security of the device itself is outside the app's control.

9. Policy changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be stated in the app or in the associated project documentation.
